What’s next: welcome to amateur radio

Congratulations on passing. Here is what happens between your exam and your call sign, and some good first steps once it arrives.

Passed with us? Leave a review so the next candidate knows what to expect.

For 30 days after your license or upgrade is granted, do not change anything in your FCC records, such as your address, name or email, and do not apply for a vanity call sign.

New license: pay the FCC’s $35 fee

  1. Watch for the FCC’s email. Once the FCC starts processing your application, it emails payment instructions to the address on your Form 605. FCC mail often does not reach Gmail, so check spam and junk as well.
  2. Check CORES too. From two business days after your exam, log in to FCC CORES and look for your application and the payment option, even if no email has arrived.
  3. Pay within 10 days, following the FCC’s payment instructions. If the fee is not paid in time, the FCC dismisses the application and you have to start again. New licenses are issued in order of payment, so paying promptly is the best way to avoid delays.

Under 18? If you tested with us, the ARRL’s Youth Licensing Grant may pay the fee back.

Upgrade: no fee

There is currently no FCC fee to upgrade. Only new licenses, renewals and call sign changes have one. Your upgrade is processed once any verification is complete.

For everyone

  • Felony conviction? Send the required documents directly to the FCC. This is strictly between you and the FCC; we cannot help or intervene.
  • How long it takes: your call sign may appear in the FCC ULS database within days, or it may take several weeks when the FCC has a backlog, as after system outages or a government shutdown.
  • Who to contact, and when:
    • No email from the FCC ten days after your exam? Email vetesting@yahoo.com and we will check on your application.
    • Paid, but no license ten days after paying? Call the FCC at (888) 225-5322.
    • Otherwise, please do not contact the FCC, ARRL or us about your paperwork. The FCC’s call center is run by a contractor and may not have accurate information.

Getting started

The most important thing you can do now is keep learning and stay active in the community.

  • Join a local amateur radio club and meet other hams: find an ARRL-affiliated club.
  • Go to hamfests and local events.
  • Consider joining the ARRL for resources and advocacy.
  • Before buying equipment, find out what others in your area use, so your choices serve you for years. Low-cost handhelds such as Baofeng or Wouxun can be difficult to program and have limited range. eHam reviews can help you compare.
  • Join PARC. PARC Radio & Technology meets monthly on Zoom, with amateur radio news, helpful tips and a presentation on an interesting topic. Meeting details. Join us as well as your local club; we would love to have you.

Join our Telegram group

We use Telegram for club news and contest announcements. Set up your profile like this so members can recognize you:

  • Name: the name you go by, followed by your call sign — for example, Gary K4XYZ. The last name can stay blank.
  • Username: @Firstname_Callsign — for example, @Gary_K4XYZ. Others can then find you without your phone number.
  • Profile photos: visible to Everybody.

Join the Telegram group

New to Telegram? Step-by-step setup
  1. Install Telegram. On an iPhone, from the App Store; on Android, from Google Play (search for Telegram Messenger); on a computer, download the desktop app from telegram.org.
  2. Create your account. Open Telegram, choose Start Messaging, and enter your mobile number and the code sent to you by text.
  3. Set your name as shown above when Telegram asks. To change it later: Settings → tap your name → Edit.
  4. Set your username: Settings → Username → enter it in the format above → Save.
  5. Show your profile photo: Settings → Privacy and Security → Profile Photos → Everybody.
  6. Hide your phone number (recommended): Settings → Privacy and Security → Phone Number. Set “Who can see my phone number” to Nobody and “Who can find me by my number” to My Contacts.
  7. Join the group with the button above. If you are signed in to Telegram, it opens automatically.

After your exam: restore your computer

To take the exam, you changed some settings and closed programs you would normally keep running. Start by shutting the computer down completely and turning it back on. That reloads your normal startup programs: the system tray on Windows, Finder and the menu bar on a Mac.

On any computer

  • Browser: close the window you used for the exam.
  • Bluetooth: turn it back on if you normally use it.
  • Do Not Disturb or Focus: turn it back off.

Windows

  • Desktop icons: right-click the desktop → View → Show desktop icons.

Mac

  • Desktop icons: restore your normal desktop icon display.
  • Stacks: click the desktop → View → uncheck Use Stacks if you normally keep individual icons.
  • Applications: reopen anything that was force-quit during the exam.
