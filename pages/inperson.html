In-person exam sessions

We hold in-person exams in Roanoke and Auburn, Alabama, and at other locations when published or by request. Every session is by appointment: we only travel to a session when a candidate has a confirmed place.

How in-person sessions work

  • Exams use the ARRL Volunteer Examiner system and the standard question pools used for all ham radio exams.
  • A published date means at least three volunteer examiners are available that day. If nobody books, nobody goes, so never travel to a session without a confirmed appointment.
  • We are happy to test one person or a hundred. Unless it is at a hamfest, a session is held because someone signed up, so you may be the only candidate.
  • Need a different date or time, or have a special request? Ask; we may be able to help.

Book an appointment

Copy the lines below into an email to vetesting@yahoo.com and fill them in. Say exactly which session you are registering for: the location, date and time.

Name:

Mobile phone number:

Current call sign:

Date & time of exam you are registering for:
Special requests:

Please attach a recent photo or selfie if you can. We do not know what you look like, and it helps us find you at the session.

Your appointment is confirmed only when we reply, usually within a day or so. If you have not heard back, email again: without a confirmed appointment, nobody may be scheduled to be there, and the session can be canceled without notice.

What to bring

  • A government-issued photo ID.
  • $15 in exact cash for each person testing; we cannot give change. The ARRL sets the fee each year. If you do not pass, we can sometimes give you a different exam for an additional fee.
  • Upgrading? Your license or any earlier CSCE, and a copy for us to keep.
  • An ink pen and a No. 2 pencil. A clipboard helps if the session is outdoors.
  • If you like, a basic calculator with no memory or programmable functions. Scratch work goes on the back of the answer sheet.

At the session

  • Most sessions are at a table in a public place such as a restaurant, away from other diners as much as possible. Look for a table with an open black briefcase. The staff may know nothing about the testing, so do not worry if you ask and they do not.
  • You are welcome to eat and drink while you test.
  • Some sessions are outdoors, and weather can change the date, time or place.
  • Anyone who comes with you waits in your vehicle, not at the session.
  • Turn your phone off completely and put it away. No calls, texts or looking at a phone or smartwatch during the exam.
  • Take your time: an exam takes anywhere from 5 minutes to half an hour. Teams can usually stay for up to an hour; ask in advance if you may need longer, or want to take another element at the same session.
  • We arrive at the time specified. If we are delayed, we call or email you. GPS coordinates for the location (DD.MM.MMM) are available on request.
  • If you pass, you receive your CSCE at the session.

Time zones

Exam times are local to the testing location. Sessions in Alabama are on Central Time, except in Valley, Alabama, which uses Eastern Time, as do all sessions in Georgia. Sessions in other states follow the local time there.

If your plans change

Please let us know as early as you can, and at least 24 hours before. We cannot reach every examiner on the day, and you may be the only person testing. Think how you would feel if you drove an hour to work to find it canceled.

About the team

We have been giving exams to new and experienced hams for many years. The group began with one family, a husband, wife and three sons, giving tests to help others enjoy this wonderful hobby. It now includes volunteers in Roanoke, Valley, Alexander City, Auburn, Anniston and Huntsville in Alabama, and Columbus and LaGrange in Georgia, and we test at Southern Union and Auburn University. Every team member is an Extra class ham and can test for every license class.

We also help with testing nationwide and are registered with several testing groups, including ARRL, W5YI, CAVEC, Alaska, Laurel and many others. We offer classes and tests on request, as well as our regular dates in Alabama and Georgia. All of us volunteer our time, and we look forward to meeting you and welcoming you to amateur radio.
